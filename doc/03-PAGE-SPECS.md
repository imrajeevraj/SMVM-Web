# SMVM Web Portal — Page Specifications

## 1. Home

### Objective

Communicate what SMVM builds and direct visitors toward products or contact.

### Sections

```text
Hero
↓
Product ecosystem
↓
Capability/value section
↓
CamBill POS showcase
↓
Product screenshots
↓
Company philosophy
↓
Vision preview
↓
Proof/trust
↓
CTA
↓
Footer
```

### Hero requirements

- clear H1
- supporting copy
- two CTAs maximum
- real product visual
- responsive layout
- no excessive decoration

---

## 2. Products

### Objective

Let visitors understand the product ecosystem quickly.

Sections:

- hero
- product filters/categories if needed
- product grid
- featured product
- future roadmap teaser only if factually supported
- CTA

---

## 3. CamBill POS

### Objective

Explain the product in enough detail to support trial/demo conversion.

Sections:

```text
Hero
Product demo
Problem
Solution
Features
Workflow
Inventory
Billing
Reports
Backup
Users/Roles
Screenshots
FAQ
CTA
```

### CTA hierarchy

Primary:

```text
Start Free Trial
```

Secondary:

```text
Contact Us
```

Only show a trial CTA if the trial actually exists.

## Cross-Page Requirements

Every page:

- responsive
- accessible
- SEO metadata
- correct heading hierarchy
- consistent header/footer

- clear CTA
- meaningful empty/error states
